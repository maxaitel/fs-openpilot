How to read the evidence

Magenta is the model plan and its uncalibrated scale. Green is an offline privileged expert reference using the custom bicycle model. Map dots are simulator ground truth; detection rings and image boxes come from the recorded model call. Ground truth and the oracle are not vision predictions.

Every video plays at 20 simulation frames per second. The final two seconds show the terminal result and explicitly contain no current prediction. Pass means the original referee accepted the run. Clean additionally requires zero geometric cone penalties and zero off-course events. PhysX contacts are reported separately.

The controller still receives speed, nominal calibration, mission progress and route commands. This is not a vision-only system. All 41 case identities, checkpoint, original referee and controller match the archived custom evaluation. RTX rendering, scenery, physical dynamics and trajectories differ. These single runs do not establish real-world safety or statistical superiority.
