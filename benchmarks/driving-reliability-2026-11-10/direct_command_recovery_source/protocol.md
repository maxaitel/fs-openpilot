# Direct-command on-policy recovery v1

This is data collection only. The frozen direct-command step 500 package failed every scheduled offline validation checkpoint. Its skidpad 82000 pilot produced 120 frames, three physical hits at 4.0, 4.85 and 5.65 seconds, and one off-course event. At frame 60 its steer was -0.0317 rad versus the teacher's -0.1475 rad on the actual state; at frame 70 it was -0.1319 versus -0.3229 rad. The acceleration error at frame 60 was +1.56 m/s². The r=0 perturbation changed frame-70 steer only about 0.015 rad, much less than the 0.191 rad steer discrepancy. This protocol does not attribute the failure primarily to an r shortcut. CPU raw-command replay passed; CPU base-head numeric replay failed; full CUDA replay passed. See ../direct_command_diagnosis/pilot.py and ../direct_command_trial/ for the evidence and frozen implementation.

Use only the two new TRAIN case IDs, copies of the matched 82000 cases in the original membership. The original membership and all trial artifacts remain frozen. Never run final scenes under this protocol. Load step_500.pt with SHA-256 9112e138d4856fbbdc383d8f63dfbaeb92fb62449c4b66b5eb6f30b91ef22ba7. This rejected checkpoint is used to generate corrective data, not as a successful model.

Start the learner from standing and process every 20 Hz observation with full history. Capture every native frame at 20 Hz as in collect.py, retain phase-zero model images and pooled desires at 5 Hz. Before the first physical contact, off-course event or invalid route, hand control to the native pure-pursuit teacher at the first |lateral| >= 0.35 m or |path-heading error| >= 0.1 rad. The teacher takes the triggering tick itself and then owns the entire suffix. Record the learner proposal, input image hashes and actual state at every prefix tick, including the handoff tick. Preserve copies of both processed RGB inputs at 20 Hz through the handoff in uint8 prefix arrays. Store the complete float32 image-head outputs as six named NPY arrays, one per camera and head leaf. Each proposal points to its frame index. The metadata binds each array name, shape and SHA-256. The verifier requires every leaf at every prefix tick, replays every prefix observation through the frozen model on CUDA by default and compares the recorded outputs at the existing 1e-5 tolerance. It checks each phase-zero packed image against the corresponding retained RGB input and reconstructs route-command desires from the realized mission and states. It checks every pooled desire across the prefix, teacher suffix and terminal continuation against the 20 Hz reconstructed sequence, including the handoff tick when it is phase zero. A clean finish without a trigger by 6 s for skidpad or 20 s for trackdrive is unqualified. Stop immediately on a dirty prefix. No counterfactual labels or extrapolated future states are allowed.

Teacher parameters are v_cap 8, a_lat_max 4, pure_pursuit, k_lookahead 0.25, with the remaining ExpertParams defaults. A qualified episode must finish, pass and complete with zero physical contacts, zero displaced cones and zero off-course events through terminal continuation. Skidpad must independently establish valid right, right, left, left, exit and stop. It must have at least eight qualified NativeEpisode anchors in the first two seconds after handoff, and stay within |lateral| <= 0.2 m and |heading error| <= 0.1 rad continuously for 0.5 s within four seconds. Append 200 real native braking ticks only after a clean finished pass. Each pre-step speed must be below 0.5 m/s. Each raw steering request must equal the previous actual steering angle and the first terminal actual angle, and each acceleration request must equal -2 m/s². Teacher flags and controls cover the actual executed suffix and braking. Verify independently before use. The receipt binds arrays, episode metadata, source archive, exact checkpoint, collector, verifier, protocol, membership and case. Trackdrive binds the exact loaded cache bytes in the source archive, hashed before loading and after collection. The verifier reconstructs the case from those bytes and checks realized cones and referee results independently. Existing RecoverySampler ignores this explicitly different mode. A future trainer must separately validate and sample it.

Keep at least 25 GiB free and at most 1 GiB per case. Use fresh output directories; retain failures and partial data. Execute cases serially after the parent has finished diagnostic runs. A successful receipt qualifies data only and makes no driving or training performance claim.
