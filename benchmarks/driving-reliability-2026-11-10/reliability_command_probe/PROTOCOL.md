# Frozen command probe protocol

Decision: determine whether changing route-command history changes this frozen policy's predicted motion on matched recorded images. This is an open-loop sensitivity probe, not a driving evaluation.

Two designs were considered before implementation. (A) Rebuild 25 hidden inputs directly from sparse native 5 Hz packed images and use their already-pooled command arrays. It is economical and can use a clean teacher lap, but these arrays contain neither the original 20 Hz RGB nor the original unpooled 100 commands. The checkpoint has no saved output on those teacher images, so it cannot meet recorded-output parity. (B) Run the original ModelPolicy sequentially from frame zero on the saved baseline, capture its policy arguments at each anchor and branch only after comparing recorded and replayed action, prediction, image head and raw policy output. Design B preserves the actual inference and controller history and permits the strongest baseline gate. It is selected. Its limitation is that this episode has no clean left-side anchors.

The source is `runs/development_head_trial1/baseline/heavy_skid`. The first contact is frame 181, while the second right lap ends at 22.12498 s, frame 443. Thus a precontact right-to-left transition or steady-left anchor does not exist in this episode. The frozen anchors are 120 (early right) and 170 (steady right), both precontact, plus 430 (approaching the right-to-left transition) and 600 (left-circle area). Frames 430 and 600 follow earlier contacts, and the run never completes an accepted left lap. No clean transition or steady-left claim is permitted. The clean TRAIN data at `data/native/lab_v2/train-skidpad-82000-matched` can support a separate follow-up, but its stored data do not establish 100 raw command samples or recorded checkpoint prediction parity. Do not combine the two as if equivalent.

Replay every recorded frame from zero through 600 without reset. For each frame compare the saved action, prediction, image head and both policy_raw outputs at rtol=atol=1e-5, exact for integer arrays. Fail closed before computing any interventions if any comparison fails. Verify source, checkpoint, graph, realization, sealed evidence and model-state provenance. Capture at anchors the actual model policy inputs [1,25,512], [1,25,8] and traffic, and a copy of the PI controller immediately before that frame's controller step. For each branch use identical hidden inputs and controller state. The controller receives the same recorded observation and mission context. The intervention is applied to the entire raw command history from frame zero using a separate TemporalState for each condition. The variants are recorded, recorded-repeat, left/right swapped across all timesteps, zero, and pulse-right-every-20-frames and pulse-left-every-20-frames. The repeated recorded condition tests reproducibility. Pulses are one frame wide and start at frame zero, with all other channels zero. Every queue is passed through the real TemporalState.push_desire. The output stores both the last 100 transformed raw commands and the 100 derived pulses. The test compares its recorded pooled queue with the captured actual queue before branching.

Save the complete raw and pooled histories, raw plan and desire-state outputs, full ego-frame plan and standard deviation, and deployed PI steering, acceleration, and diagnostic motion values. Save SHA-256 for script, protocol, metadata, manifest, completion receipt, summary, every consumed evidence frame, checkpoint, source, graphs and model tensor digest before and after. Do not interpret or report effects unless all gates pass. Motion responses are instantaneous counterfactuals at fixed states and controller memory, not a closed-loop trajectory. They cannot establish lap completion, contact avoidance, or generalization.
