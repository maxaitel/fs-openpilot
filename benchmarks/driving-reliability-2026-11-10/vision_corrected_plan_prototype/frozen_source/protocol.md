# Frozen constant-offset prototype

The goal is reliable vision driving on full trackdrive and full RRLL skidpad with zero physical cone contacts. This throwaway CPU prototype asks whether scored cone boundary offsets correct the frozen neural plan's nearfield target on the two authorized TRAIN precontact prefixes. It does not test physical driving. The final case stays unopened.

The parent selected a small plan-prior correction after direct actuator fitting and the two-sided gate seed failed. The chosen data shape is one immutable Observation with plan XY, scored cone tuples, timestamp, measured vx/vy/yaw rate, and mission kind. Stateful cone tracks contain only ego coordinates, age, class, score, and an orange side. The runtime receives no pose, heading, progress, centerline, actual cones, or teacher commands. The old NN prior already consumes supplied external route commands. The prototype uses no extra progress-derived input. AS launch is not implemented and cannot override unknown braking.

One module owns transport, association, boundary residuals, path offsets, and pure pursuit. The separate replay owns evidence validation and privileged scoring. The existing frozen scorer supplies canonical teacher geometry after reproducing its cached controls within 1e-10. This small greenfield interface and selected approach do not require a new architectural design contest. No additional agents are spawned for this bounded delegate task.

Use all 80 frames strictly before direct pilot contact and all 53 frames strictly before recovery pilot contact. `read_evidence` validates each selected frame and image hash, while the completion receipt binds the full manifest and summary. All four modulo-four control phases remain in every aggregate and are also reported separately. Metadata must bind the original base state `14feb933...` at 20 Hz. Joint-policy plans and other recordings are excluded.

The route prior is the first 12 m of saved plan XY, resampled spatially every 0.25 m. Degenerate segments shorter than 0.01 m are removed. It is never re-anchored at the origin. Only scored observations at radial range 5 to 10 m and x at least zero enter tracks. Score 0.55 is an explicit engineering filter and is not calibrated confidence. Tracks are transported by the previous measured constant body twist, associated greedily within 0.6 m, and expire 1.5 s after the last eligible observation. Updates outside that range do not overwrite transported positions. Gaps over 0.3 s or non-increasing timestamps reset memory. Conflicting classes become unknown obstacles until expiry. Orange and big orange are compatible lane classes.

Project each track onto the current neural prior. Blue is left and yellow is right. Orange takes its first consistent relative side when normal distance exceeds 0.5 m. Require its side to stay consistent. A side distance below 0.25 m or a width residual larger than 2 m cannot support correction. The residual is signed normal distance minus signed nominal halfwidth. Nominal halfwidth is an engineering assumption of 1.5 m for skidpad and 1.75 m for trackdrive, selected by mission kind without reading actual width. A weighted median uses score, exp of negative age divided by 1.5 s, and exp of negative station divided by 6 m. Clip the single fitted offset to 0.75 m. A constant offset is chosen before evaluation because a heading fit can be underdetermined by a single boundary cone. No two-sided nearfield seed is required. One usable observation is enough for conditional support and never establishes obstacle completeness or a free corridor.

Shift the prior along its sampled normal by that constant offset, including the first point. Use spatial lookahead 1.8 + 0.25 times nonnegative measured vx. Rear-axle pure pursuit uses wheelbase 1.55 m and rear offset 0.75 m. The uncorrected comparison receives the same resampled prior, measured motion, support state, tracked obstacles, and speed rule. Both expose a geometric steering request on every usable path, plus the actual request which is zero when unsupported. The old raw actuator head is the third reference.

The speed target is capped at 5 m/s, 2.5 m/s² curvature acceleration, and a stopping-distance heuristic with 3 m/s² deceleration and 0.3 s delay. The farthest support station minus a front extent and pad provides its distance. A single observed cone does not establish clear space. This distance is only a conservative request heuristic under unvalidated assumptions. A sampled rectangle conflict with any carried cone or fresh score-0.25 observation brakes. The rectangle uses front 1.6 m, rear 1.3 m, width 1.4 m, cone radius 0.15 m, and pad 0.2 m. No native plant accuracy or MPC safety claim is made. Unknown support always requests negative acceleration, including at rest.

The geometry screen is frozen in config.json. Each episode must have at least 90 percent support, all-candidate and supported lateral target MAE at most 0.10 m and p95 at most 0.25 m, improved all-candidate lateral MAE against uncorrected PP, no regression in teacher steering-direction agreement where absolute teacher steer exceeds 0.03 rad, no supported heading-error branch proxy over 0.6 rad, and zero supported sampled GT footprint conflicts. The full proposed 12 m path supplies the footprint screen. These are provisional design screens, not existing raw-actuator qualification thresholds. Teacher acceleration error is diagnostic only. Branch semantics and all physical, route-completion, VAL, and frozen-final gates remain unchanged.

Ground truth remains in the replay/scorer. Score canonical teacher target geometry, steering direction, raw steering and diagnostic acceleration error, all-candidate and supported path margins, and each GT cone margin including the first-hit cone. Save complete support diagnostics and every per-cone margin. Report the same frames for both geometric variants. Source, protocol, config, selection inputs, and canonical scorer dependencies are hashed before the first replay output. Freeze forbids post-result recipe changes. Repeating the process must produce identical result and rows bytes.

Throughput checkpoint. CPU only, 133 permitted frames, two geometry variants and one raw-command reference. No inference, training, GPU, SSH, simulator, held-out case, or production edit. Input decoding and geometric scoring bound the run. Synthetic behavior checks cover body-motion transport, memory expiry/reset, nonzero initial offset, side/sign, class conflict, and unknown braking. These checks are explicitly required by the parent and are the exception to the throwaway playbook's usual no-tests preference.
