# Frozen detector quality protocol

Inputs are the full recorded frames and compact references in runs/vision_corridor_probe_v1. Hash every consumed input before and after, validate copy receipt, producer receipt and metadata array hashes. No model, simulation, tuning, or planner use of reference geometry. All phase-zero 5 Hz recorded frames are scored. Terminal continuation means tick >= terminal_continuation.start_tick. Skidpad route phases use only source crossing timestamps, with entry before first crossing, four laps between crossings, exit afterward. Trackdrive has no route subdivision.

Associate separately within the blue/yellow group and orange/big-orange group, as the prior blue/yellow scorer excludes orange. Within each group use Hungarian assignment minimizing Euclidean distance with edges at >=0.75 m assigned cost 1e6; then retain only edges strictly <0.75 m. Class must match exactly for correct association. Predictions are finite stored detections, without additional score filtering. Prediction ranges are radial ego distances [0,5), [5,10), [10,20) m. Report correct matches / predictions, mismatched assignments, and median and p95 distances only among correct matches inside the gate. This is conditional truncation, not uncertainty. No frustum recall is reported.

Per frame and range, count all predicted blue and yellow independently, confidence bands <0.5, [0.5,0.75), >=0.75, and forward x>0 blue/yellow counts. Both-color support means at least one forward blue and yellow prediction in that range, without claiming geometric pairing or correctness. Summarize frames lacking both-color support and longest consecutive gap for driving and terminal separately. Store each frame's counts. These descriptive bands are fixed in advance, not optimized for driving. Assess whether a streaming planner can rely on these observed frames, not safety or generalization. Frames are correlated and only two TRAIN seeds.
